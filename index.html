What we invest in

Investment Criteria

The criteria are mechanical on purpose. They keep the fund out of judgment calls between classmates and index it toward the strongest rounds coming out of the class.

01

A founder is Class of 2028

At least one founder is, or was, a member of the HBS Class of 2028.

  • A founder holds more than 10% of the company.
  • Classmates who leave the program after their RC year still count.

02

A qualified firm leads the round

The round is led by a venture firm on a closed list the fund fixes before it starts investing.

  • The list is built with our advisory board and shared with LPs once we have raised.
  • Leading VC performs diligence and sets price.

03

The round is $500K or larger

Rounds below that threshold are out of scope.

  • Filters out scout checks and informal friends-and-family rounds.
  • Keeps our ownership meaningful at a $10K to $50K check.

Questions

Before you commit.

For investors

How many VC-backed founders does an HBS class produce?

Historically more than eighty students in a class start a company within three months of graduating, which is more than any other business school produces in that window, Stanford included.

Some share of those companies go on to raise a qualifying round of $500K or more from a top-tier firm. Those are the ones the fund can invest in.

How did you build the list of qualified venture firms?

It is a closed list, put together with help from the investors on our advisory board. We share it with LPs once the fund has raised.

Are there kinds of companies that never qualify?

Yes. The fund does not invest in non-profits, academic research, grant-funded work, or search funds.

How are investments approved? Is voting weighted by how much I put in?

The fund is structured as an investment club, so LPs vote on each investment we bring to them. Voting is not weighted. Every LP gets one equal vote regardless of commitment size.

  • You can opt in to all future investments automatically when you register.
  • Reminders to vote go out by email and text for each individual deal.
  • LPs are expected to vote on at least two-thirds of investments, and may risk forfeiting their position if they do not.

At Stanford's class fund and others like it, no LP has ever voted a deal down.

Do you make follow-on investments?

We reserve capital for one follow-on per company, sized at 50% to 100% of the initial check where that check carried pro-rata rights. A follow-on has to clear three conditions:

  • The new round is led by an institutional investor.
  • We hold pro-rata rights from the original investment.
  • The follow-on is no more than 2.5% of the fund.
How do distributions work in a liquidity event?

We follow the funds we invest alongside. Proceeds are not recycled into new investments. They are distributed pro rata to every LP.

What happens to capital we never deploy?

Undeployed capital is returned to LPs pro rata.

Is the team paid?

No. There is no management fee and no carried interest.

We are doing this for the class and for the founders in it. We will also admit to the less selfless reasons: real investing experience, and a much stronger network among our classmates, the founders in our year, and the investors backing them.

How do capital calls work?

There is a single capital call, taken upfront when you commit. You will not be called on again later in the fund's life.

Will I get regular updates?

Yes. LPs receive our founder newsletter through the year, plus an annual report on the fund and its portfolio.

Are there legal limits on who can be an LP?

Anyone in the HBS Class of 2028 can invest. There is no visa restriction and no accreditation requirement.

Can I sell my interest or exit early?

No. There is no secondary market for LP interests in the fund.

For founders

I am raising in the next year. How do I reach you?

Email us at twenty28ventures@gmail.com.

What does Twenty28 do for me beyond the check?

Think of us as the most useful friends-and-family check on your cap table. We ask for very little equity, and in exchange you get:

  • Direct introductions to partners at top-tier firms through our advisory board.
  • An automatic $10K to $50K commitment, which signals interest and helps you get to an oversubscribed round faster.
  • Term sheet advice when you need a second opinion.
  • A channel to share company updates with the whole class, which turns into customers and hires.
Do you lead rounds? What is a typical check?

We do not lead. Checks run from $10,000 to $50,000 alongside a qualified lead.

Could foreign capital in the fund create sanctions or CFIUS problems for me?

No. The LP list stays private and is never published, and the fund closes at graduation, so its investments are treated as U.S.-based.

We do invest in qualifying companies outside the U.S. under the same rules-based criteria, reviewed case by case and in line with how the wider investor community treats them.
